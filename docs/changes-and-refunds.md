# Changes and refunds

Northwind Air is a fictional airline used for this demo.

## Changing a flight

You can change the date of a flight up to 2 hours before departure. Economy fares have a change fee of AUD 50 plus any difference in fare. Business fares can be changed for free.

Same-day changes can only be made at the airport, if seats are available.

## Cancelling and refunds

Flexible fares can be refunded in full if cancelled more than 24 hours before departure. Saver fares can't be refunded, but the value can be kept as travel credit for 12 months.

Refunds go back to the original payment method and take up to 10 business days.

## Name changes

Names on a booking can't be transferred to another person. Spelling corrections of up to three letters are free.
