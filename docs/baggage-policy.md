# Baggage policy

Northwind Air is a fictional airline used for this demo.

## Checked baggage

Every economy fare includes one checked bag up to 23 kg. Business fares include two checked bags up to 32 kg each.

Extra checked bags cost AUD 70 each when added online, or AUD 95 at the airport.

## Cabin baggage

You can bring one cabin bag up to 7 kg and one small personal item, such as a handbag or laptop bag. Cabin bags must fit in the overhead locker.

## Special items

Sporting equipment, such as surfboards and golf clubs, counts as one checked bag if it weighs 23 kg or less. Musical instruments can travel in the cabin if a seat is booked for them.
