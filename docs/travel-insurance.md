# Travel insurance

Northwind Air is a fictional airline used for this demo.

Travel insurance is optional and can be added when you book or up to 7 days before departure.

## What it covers

- Overseas medical costs, including emergency treatment and hospital stays
- Trips cancelled because of illness, injury or a family emergency
- Lost, stolen or damaged baggage, up to AUD 3,000 per person

## What it doesn't cover

Pre-existing medical conditions aren't covered unless you declare them and they're accepted. Trips cancelled because you changed your mind aren't covered.

## Making a claim

Claims must be made within 30 days of returning home, with receipts and any medical reports.
